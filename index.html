Day to day.

What you check most, one screen at a time.

Dashboard

How full. How far. How recent.

Riviera shows how old the latest reading is. On an electric car, you also see the charge level and the charging time left.3

Controls

Horn, lights and climate, from your phone.

What works depends on your model. Climate needs a plug-in or electric car.

Sound the horn, flash the lights or start climate pre-conditioning. Choose the cabin temperature, or schedule when it starts.

Which cars accept horn and lights?

Mostly cars with OpenR Link, the touchscreen system in newer Renaults (about 2022 onward), such as the Austral and Mégane E-Tech. Most older Easy Link and R-Link 2 cars refuse the command, and Riviera tells you so. Settings › Help & Support › Supported Vehicles shows the system for each model.

Lease

Mileage against your allowance.

Days left and a projection to the end of the contract. The estimated cost of going over, too.

Costs

What the car costs.

Each month, and per kilometre or mile. Fuel and maintenance, next to lease, insurance and inspection.

Documents

What is due, and when.

Your licence, inspection, insurance and road tax or vignette. Add the dates. Riviera reminds you before they fall due.

Parking

Back to your car.

A full-screen map of the last position your car reported. Directions opens your preferred maps app.

The finer points.

The history behind the numbers. And your car, without opening the app.

Fill-ups

Every fill-up, in one place.

Type a fill-up or scan a Romanian or English receipt, then follow consumption and fuel cost.

Mileage

Your odometer, over time.

Distance this year and the monthly average, on a chart.

Service Schedule

Every service item, on one list.

Tyres, brake fluid, filters and more, each with a due date, the distance left and a tap to add it to Calendar and Reminders.

Lock status

The last report, with its time.

The door lock card shows the last status your car reported, and when, on the few models that report it.

Parking History

Where you parked before.

Up to 90 days of places your car reported, kept on your iPhone and named as you like.

Widgets

Parked, on your Home Screen.

The Parking Location widget shows fuel, range and, if you allow it, the parking address, with Directions a tap away.

Shortcuts

Set it up once.

Scan the receipt at the pump from the Action button or a shortcut, or add the price later without opening Riviera.

Privacy

Your records stay with you.

On your iPhone, plus any backups and features you switch on.

No server of its own. No analytics. No ads.

  • No server of its own

    Riviera has no server of its own, no analytics and no ads.

  • Your password

    Your My Renault password goes only to Renault’s sign-in service. Riviera never saves it.

  • Your records

    Your records stay on your iPhone, and in the iPhone backups you make, unless you switch on a feature that copies them, such as iCloud Sync, Shared Garage or Apple TV.

  • Your location

    Fuel, charger and dealer searches send a position rounded to about 100 metres to the public services that answer them. Weather lookups send a position rounded to about 10 kilometres. Apple Maps and Apple’s address lookup, which Riviera also uses, receive exact coordinates. The policy lists every service.

  • Erasing

    Erase what Riviera keeps on your iPhone and in your own iCloud from inside the app, without asking the developer. Backups, a Shared Garage you joined and data held by Renault are not touched. The policy lists what remains.

  • The developer

    Riviera itself sends nothing to the developer. What reaches the developer is what you choose to email, plus anonymous crash and usage figures that Apple shares with developers if you allow it in iOS.

The short version. The full privacy policy is the one to rely on.

Good to know.

What to expect.

  • Renault cars only.

    Your car needs to be connected to a My Renault account. What shows depends on your market, model year, connected-services subscription and privacy mode. Riviera hides the readings your car does not report.

    They come from Renault’s servers. Riviera is not a safety system. Its deadlines are estimates and reminders, not legal advice.

Questions.

Short answers. More in the app, under Settings › Help & Support.

Is Riviera an official Renault app?

No. Riviera is independent and unofficial. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Renault s.a.s. It uses the same sign-in and vehicle-data services as the official My Renault app, with your own account.

Can Riviera lock or unlock my car?

No. Renault’s service has no such command. Riviera shows only the last lock status your car reported, with the time of that report. It can sound the horn and flash the lights where Renault allows that for your car.

What happens to my password?

Riviera is not Renault’s own app, so you type your My Renault password into a third-party app. You type it only on Riviera’s sign-in screen. It goes only to Renault’s sign-in service, and Riviera does not save it. Riviera keeps Renault’s sign-in token, your email address and your account IDs, among a few other items listed in the privacy policy, in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone. iOS itself may offer to save the password in your own password manager. That is your choice.

Why doesn’t my car’s data update every minute?

Riviera reads your car through Renault’s servers, which limit how often an account may ask. The car also needs a mobile signal. Pulling down on the Dashboard asks Renault for the latest reading, at most once every 30 seconds.

Which cars does Riviera work with?

Renault cars that appear in your My Renault account. Features vary by model. Climate and charging controls need a plug-in or electric car. On some newer electric models, such as the Renault 4 and 5, Renault does not accept the charging commands Riviera sends, so charging features are limited on those models. Some markets have no connected services at all.

What does Riviera cost?

Riviera is free: no ads, no subscription and no paid features. Renault’s connected services are separate and may have their own terms.
