Riviera is an independent, unofficial companion app for Renault connected cars, made by an individual developer. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Renault s.a.s. Riviera is free and has no paid features.

Contact

Developer: Ionuț Andrei Paraschiv, Romania
Write in English or Romanian. Every message is read by the developer, usually within a few working days.

Riviera cannot help with your My Renault account, your car, its connected services or its subscription: for those, contact Renault or your Renault dealer.

What the app does

Sign in with your own My Renault account and Riviera shows your car's latest fuel or battery level, range, odometer, lock state and parked position as Renault reports them, and sends the commands Renault offers for your car (horn and lights, and climate or charging on cars that support them). It also keeps your fill-ups, services, repairs, lease or credit, documents and tyre sets, with reminders for due dates, and finds fuel prices, EV chargers and Renault dealers nearby. Riviera has no server of its own, no analytics and no ads: your records stay on your iPhone unless you turn on a feature that copies them, such as iCloud Sync.

Frequently asked questions

How do I sign in?

Use the same e-mail address and password as in the My Renault app. Your password goes only to Renault's sign-in service and Riviera never saves it. If Riviera cannot sign you in, check first that you can sign in to the My Renault app with the same details. Renault ends a session after about 30 days, and Riviera then asks for your password again.

Why does my car's data not update every minute?

Riviera reads your car through Renault's servers, which limit how often an account may ask. To stay inside those limits, Riviera makes at most 45 automatic reads an hour, and the Refresh Car Data action in Shortcuts runs at most once every 10 minutes. If Renault answers that there were too many requests, automatic refresh pauses for 15 minutes. Pulling down on the Dashboard asks Renault for the latest reading, at most once every 30 seconds. The car also needs a mobile signal, and a reading from a car that is asleep can take a few minutes to arrive.

Can I try Riviera without a Renault car?

Yes. On the sign-in screen, tap Try it with a sample car. Demo Mode shows one fictional plug-in hybrid with sample records and needs no account. Changes you make to the sample car are not saved. Once signed in, you can also turn it on with the Demo Mode switch in Settings → Help & Support → Sample Car. To leave, tap the "Demo car" line on the Dashboard and choose Exit Demo Mode, or use Exit Demo Mode at the top of Settings.

How do I delete my data?

In Settings → Privacy & Security, tap Delete All My Data. It erases what Riviera keeps on your iPhone and in your iCloud, including the Shared Garage you own. It cannot delete your Renault account or the data Renault holds, backups of your iPhone, or files you saved elsewhere. The privacy policy lists exactly what it removes and what it leaves. To have an e-mail you sent to the developer deleted, write to the address above.

My car model is not listed, or a feature is missing

Riviera works with Renault cars linked to a My Renault account; Dacia and Alpine cars are not supported. Settings → Help & Support → Supported Vehicles shows the models Riviera knows and what each one reports. A car that is not listed still shows whatever Renault sends for it. What a car can do depends on its model year, its multimedia system, your country, your connected-services subscription and Privacy Mode, so a feature missing in Riviera is often missing in the My Renault app too. If Riviera does not know your tank size, the fuel level shows in litres: set Tank Capacity in Settings → Vehicle to see a percentage. If your model is missing from the list, tell the developer (see "How to report a problem" below) so it can be looked at.

Why can't I lock or unlock the car from the app?

Renault's connected-car service offers no remote lock or unlock command for any model, so Riviera only reads the lock state, where the car reports it. Riviera never sends a lock or unlock request.

How to report a problem

In the app, open Settings → Help & Support and tap Report a Problem. Riviera prepares an e-mail to the developer with a diagnostics file attached. It shows you the whole file first: e-mail addresses, VINs, sign-in tokens, precise coordinates and account IDs are removed, and nothing is sent until you send the e-mail yourself. When the app shows a "Something went wrong" card, its Send Report button does the same with the error included. You can also write to the address above without the diagnostics. It helps to say what you tapped, what you expected and what happened, and to add a screenshot.
