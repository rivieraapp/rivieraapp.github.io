Try Riviera

Tap through pictures of the real app, using a sample car. You need no Renault account for this, and there is nothing to install.

Show the app in

Light and Dark change the app in the pictures, not this page.

How it works

  1. Tap what glows.
  2. Scroll a screen to see the rest of it.
  3. Switch the app between Light and Dark.
  4. Use the tabs at the bottom of the screen.
  5. Press Back to go back. Esc works too.
All screens

These pictures are captures of the real Riviera app, taken in an iPhone simulator, with the sample car “My Rafale”, shown with Renault’s picture of a Rafale. The pictures are not edited, apart from being cut and joined as described below. The one exception is the Renault emblem on the grille of the car in the Home Screen widgets, which is covered. In Light, the Dashboard, Controls and Finance were captured on a plain grey backdrop so that the joined pictures show no seams, and the Home Screen picture shows Apple’s own icons and wallpaper around Riviera’s widgets. Every figure, and the car’s position, is made up, and the times in the pictures, such as “1 minute ago”, do not change. Light and Dark are the app’s own two appearances on iOS.

A screen that scrolls is one tall picture, joined from several captures of the app taken at different scroll positions. The status bar at the top and the tab bar at the bottom are cut from captures of the same screen and stay in place while the content moves under them.

The real app works with Renault cars that appear in a My Renault account. This demo signs in to nothing. In the app itself, the sample car you can open without an account is named “Demo car”.
